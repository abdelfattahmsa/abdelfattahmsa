## What this site collects

**By default, nothing.** The site is static: pages are plain files, there are no accounts and no advertising.

## Analytics (only if you accept)

If you click **Accept** on the cookie notice, the site loads Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are useful: pages viewed, how far people scroll, which links and downloads are used, search terms typed into the site's search, questions asked to Ask AM! (length only), page speed and the language and theme chosen. Google sets cookies starting with `_ga` for this. Advertising features and ad personalisation are switched off, and nothing is sold. If you click **Decline**, nothing is loaded and no cookies are set. You can change your mind at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer.

## Contact form and newsletter

When you send the contact form or subscribe, your name, email and message are sent to Formspree, the form service, and delivered to my inbox. I use them only to reply to you or to send the notes you asked for.

## Ask AM!

The assistant is a search that answers in chat form. It runs entirely in your browser over the pages of this site: your questions are not sent to any server and no AI service is involved.

## Your choices

You can decline analytics, clear this site's data in your browser at any time, or write to me to ask what I hold about you.
