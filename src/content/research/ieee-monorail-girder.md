## Abstract

Developed a weak-AI decision-support software tool for selecting optimal structural systems and materials for elevated monorail structures, considering critical factors such as time and cost. The research compared multiple structural systems and presented an innovative approach to engineering decision-making using artificial intelligence.

## Why it matters

Selecting a structural system is usually driven by experience and intuition. This work makes the trade-offs explicit and comparable, so that the engineer's judgement is supported by data rather than replaced by it.
