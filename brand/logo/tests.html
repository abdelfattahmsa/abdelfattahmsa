AM signature · test sheet

A brush-pen signature monogram in capitals, AM., with the full name set underneath in widely tracked capitals. Drawn from hand-placed pen strokes (a flat nib held at a fixed angle, so down-strokes are thick and up-strokes thin) and converted to one clean filled outline. One colour, no effects. Regenerate with node brand/build-signature.mjs.
