2 · 16 px test PASS: mark MARGINAL: 16×16 favicon at 1×

Left: actual size. Right: the same pixels enlarged 8× (no smoothing). The monogram passes at 16 px tall (≈31 px wide). A square 16×16 favicon can only be ≈14 px wide, so the mark is ≈7 px tall: letters read as "AM" but the A's arch opening nearly closes. It is crisp at 32 px (2× / retina), which is what most screens use. Recommendation: have a hand pixel-hinted 16×16 favicon made (see brief).
