Mascot ideas

Black and white, geometric, one yellow accent. Each shown: large · next to the logo · 64/32/16 px · single colour on white · as an app/avatar disc.

01The Builder

Hard-hat engineer. Round head, semicircle hat, half-disc body. Says civil engineering at a glance.

02The AM Face

The letters are the character: a yellow A as the hat, an M as the mouth. Most ownable, works as a pure monogram.

03The Island

Ties to the Knowledge Island idea: a figure on a floating half-disc with a laptop and a small sun.

04The System Builder

A modular robot built from rounded blocks and one antenna dot. Speaks to software, systems and product work.

05The Half-Face

Upper half solid, lower half outline: the contrast of the logo-grid reference. Simplest of all; strongest at 16 px.

06The Totem

A stack of pure shapes like the reference logo grid: semicircle head, block torso, two legs. Reads as a pictogram.
